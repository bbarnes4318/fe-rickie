## The 9 Phases (Golden Path)

### Phase 1: The Trust Anchor (0:00 - 0:45) 👋

**Goal**: Establish authority and verify identity

**Key Points**:

- Use downward inflection on your name
- NEVER ask "How are you?" (loses -5.4%)
- Verify name, state, and age
- Get early "Fair enough?" tie-down

---

### Phase 2: The Emotional Excavation (0:45 - 4:40) 💜

**Goal**: Discover beneficiary and build emotional connection

**Key Points**:

- Ask who handles arrangements (+12.7% lift)
- Get beneficiary's name
- Ask about their financial situation
- Identify their motivation (health scare, death in family, etc.)
- Validate their concerns (+12.9% lift)

**Critical Question**:

> "God forbid, if you passed away today, would [beneficiary] have the $10,000 to $15,000 cash on hand?"

---

### Phase 3: The Eligibility Pivot (4:40 - 7:30) 🏥

**Goal**: Qualify health and build rate profile

**Key Points**:

- Permission-based approach (+16.1% lift)
- Combine questions (+10.0% lift)
- Tobacco → Height/Weight → Heart → Lungs → Diabetes → Cancer → Hospital
- Get full medication list
- Empathize throughout

---

### Phase 4: The Value Bridge (7:30 - 9:00) 📚

**Goal**: Educate on benefits and set price anchor

**Key Points**:

- Inflation story (+7.5% lift)
- First Day Coverage (vs. 2-year wait)
- Anchor $50-$80/month (+13.4% lift)
- Benefits over features (+14.7% lift)

**Power Phrase**:

> "Locks in your rate forever. Never goes up."

---

### Phase 5: The Option Selection (9:00 - 10:45) 💰

**Goal**: Present pricing and get selection

**Key Points**:

- "Grab a pen and paper" (control technique)
- Present 3 options (+9.4% lift)
- High → Mid → Low
- Use silence after (+29.1% lift - CRITICAL!)

**Silence is GOLD**: Count to 10. First person to speak loses!

---

### Phase 6: The Commitment Seal (10:45 - 12:00) ✅

**Goal**: Close and lock in decision

**Key Points**:

- Alternative choice close (+17.4% lift)
- Assumptive close (+30.3% lift)
- Verify address
- Lock in beneficiary

**Power Close**:

> "Which one fits your budget best so we can get this to [beneficiary]?"

---

### Phase 7: The Identity Lock (12:00 - 15:30) 🔒

**Goal**: Collect SSN securely

**Key Points**:

- MIB check explanation
- Direct ask
- If hesitation: "I can't see it, turns to asterisks"
- Screen share option if needed

---

### Phase 8: The Financial Disarm (15:30 - 19:00) 🏦

**Goal**: Get banking information smoothly

**Key Points**:

- Normalize the ask (+46.0% lift - HIGHEST!)
- Explain fraud prevention
- Routing number first ("verify participating bank")
- Account number next
- Align draft with SS payment date

**Critical Frame**:

> "They don't accept cash or checks anymore because of fraud."

---

### Phase 9: The Victory Lap (19:00 - 35:58) 🎉

**Goal**: Confirm, build rapport, get referral

**Key Points**:

- Congratulate them
- Summary close (+23.6% lift)
- Set expectations (7-10 days)
- Peace of mind statement (+15.6% lift)
- Referral request
- Save your contact

## Objection Handling Quick Reference

### "Too Expensive" 💰

1. Empathize: "I understand, you're on a fixed income"
2. Isolate: "Is it price or value?"
3. If price: Drop face amount immediately
4. Frame: "Some coverage better than no coverage"

### "Need to Think" 🤔

1. Empathize: "It's a decision"
2. Isolate: "Is it price or need to talk to someone?"
3. Frame: "Don't know if approved yet. 30-day free look."
4. Urgency: "48 hours, rate goes away"

### "Talk to Family" 👨‍👩‍👧

1. Validate: "Great idea to tell [beneficiary]"
2. Reframe: "If you ask them, what will they say?"
3. Truth: "They love you, don't want you to worry"
4. Close: "Let's see if approved first, then show them"

### "Don't Trust / Scam Concern" 🔐

1. Validate: "Right to be careful"
2. Credentials: Give NPN and state license
3. Text license photo
4. Department of Insurance lookup

## Script Navigation

### How Decision Tree Works

Each node has:

- **Script Text** - What to say (auto-personalized)
- **Options** - Prospect responses
- **Next Node** - Where to go next

**Example**:

```
NODE: verify_identity
Script: "I have you listed as Mary Johnson, residing in Texas, is that correct?"

Options:
  [✅ Yes] → Goes to verify_age
  [❌ No]  → Goes to correct_info
```

### Variable Substitution

All variables are **automatically replaced**:

- `{first_name}` → "Mary"
- `{beneficiary}` → "Sarah"
- `{state}` → "Texas"
- `{coverage_amount}` → "$10,000"
- `{monthly_premium}` → "$75"

You just read what's on screen - it's already personalized!
